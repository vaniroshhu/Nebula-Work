About The Course

An essential guide to theoretical modeling and hands-on algorithms for intelligence systems

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is the study of agents that receive percepts from the environment and perform actions. It aims to formalize, understand, and construct intelligent entities capable of rational behaviors.

Importance in Modern Society

From healthcare diagnostics and self-driving cars to generative transformers, AI drives technological transformation. It provides scale, reduces margins of human error, and discovers complex data configurations.

Career Opportunities

Prepares students for advanced specialized career profiles, including AI Research Scientists, Machine Learning Engineers, NLP Researchers, Autonomous Systems Developers, and Data Analysts.
